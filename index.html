The Process

Inside the
Transformation

From the moment a raw cashew arrives to the final sorted kernel, every stage demands precision. Each step exists for a reason.

01

Select

Raw cashews are assessed and selected. Uniformity at this stage determines every outcome that follows.

02

Process

Controlled conditions prepare each nut for separation. Temperature, timing, and method are calibrated with care.

03

Separate

The shell is carefully removed. The goal is always the same: preserve the whole kernel, intact and undamaged.

04

Sort

Kernels are graded by size, colour, and integrity. Consistency is not a target — it is the standard.

05

Refine

Final inspection and preparation. Each kernel is evaluated before advancing to the next stage.

06

Pack

Sealed, protected, and prepared for dispatch. What leaves the facility carries the name — and the standard.

Quality

Precision Is the
Foundation

Consistency

Every batch processed to the same exacting standard. Uniformity is not aspirational — it is operational.

Control

Monitored conditions throughout the process. Temperature, timing, and handling — measured, not estimated.

Integrity

Whole kernel recovery is the measure of skill. Broken pieces represent lost value — we minimize both.

Grading

Systematic sorting by size and quality. Accurate grading builds confidence in every shipment.

Origin

Based in Palanpur, Gujarat — at the heart of India's cashew processing corridor.

Craft

Some processes require machinery. Others require hands. Knowing the difference is the craft.

The distance between raw material and finished product is measured in precision.

At Aditya Nuts, every cashew tells the same story — a transformation guided by consistent process, careful handling, and a refusal to compromise. We do not chase volume at the expense of quality. We process with intention, and the result carries our name because it meets our standard.
