Process Overview

Cashew Transformation

A general conceptual overview showing how raw cashews transition from natural harvest shells into sorted kernels.

01

Harvest Prep

Raw cashews are prepared and conditioned to ease outer shell separation while safeguarding the inner kernel.

02

Shelling

The tough outer shell is split open to release the nut intact with careful manual or mechanical cracking.

03

Peeling

The thin outer skin (testa) is removed after drying, exposing the smooth cream-colored cashew kernel.

04

Grading

Kernels are sorted according to size, whole status, and visual specifications required by trade standards.

05

Quality Inspection

Batches are visually evaluated to ensure consistency, clean appearance, and uniform sorting.

06

Packaging

Finished cashews are sealed in protective containers to preserve quality during handling and dispatch.
